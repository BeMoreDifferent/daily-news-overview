Google Analytics

With your consent we use Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"), to understand which briefings and stories are read, for roughly how long a story is on screen while you scroll, which summaries and publisher links are opened, and how the site is used. These measurements describe the stories, not you. Google Analytics is only loaded after you make a choice in the banner.

  • If you accept: Google sets cookies (_ga, _ga_*, stored for up to 2 years) with a random identifier that recognises repeat visits. Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG.
  • If you decline: no cookies or identifiers are stored on your device. Google receives only cookieless signals (such as page address, time, browser type and screen size) that are used in aggregate for counting visits and cannot recognise you on a later visit.

In both cases Google signals and advertising features are disabled and data is not used for advertising. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses for users in the EU; it uses them only briefly to derive the approximate country. Analytics data is deleted after 2 months. Google may process data in the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). More information: Google Privacy Policy.

You can withdraw or change your choice at any time with effect for the future: open privacy settings (also linked as "Privacy settings" at the bottom of the briefing). Withdrawing consent deletes the Google Analytics cookies.

Images and links to publishers

Story images are loaded directly from the publishers' servers, which therefore receive your IP address. No referrer is sent with these requests. Links to articles take you to the publishers' sites, whose own privacy policies apply.
